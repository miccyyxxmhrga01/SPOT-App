# SPOT Privacy Policy

Draft for owner review • Prepared 11 September 2026

This draft describes the app code reviewed on this date and proposes operating practices for SPOT. The operator must verify the live Firebase settings and complete the contact, age, retention and deletion details before adopting it.

## 1. Who is responsible for your information

SPOT is developed and operated by Mic Dah Ryl Caybin in the Philippines. Privacy questions and requests: caybinmicdahryl@gmail.com. Effective date: [DATE OF ADOPTION]. The operator is responsible for deciding why and how SPOT uses your personal information.

## 2. Information used by SPOT

Account information: your email address, Firebase account identifier, authentication provider, email verification status and account timestamps. Email/password authentication is handled through Google Firebase Authentication. If you use Google sign-in, SPOT receives the account details supplied for that sign-in, such as your email, name and profile image. SPOT does not ask for your Google password.

Profile information: the account type (Student or Spotter), display name and username you choose. Students provide a course/program and their talents. Spotters may optionally provide an organization and the talents they are interested in finding. Your biography and profile photo are optional for both account types. Email signup does not require your legal or full name. Your account type is self-selected and does not establish verified student or recruiter status.

Community information: posts and associated media references, captions, tags, talent categories, timestamps and follow relationships used to display profiles and community content. Information you make visible to other users may be copied by them. Any live messaging or additional community features must be assessed and added to this policy before their data collection is enabled.

Technical information: Firebase and Google services may process identifiers, device and app information, network information and service logs. This build includes the Firebase Analytics SDK, which can collect app interaction events and device-related information. The operator must confirm its collection settings and any required choice or consent before public use.

Photos: SPOT accesses a photo you select for upload through the device's image picker. Selecting a profile photo is optional. This does not give SPOT permission to publish every photo on your device.

## 3. Why information is used

SPOT uses account information to sign you in, verify your email, maintain your session and help you recover access. Profile and community information is used to show your profile, organize content around talent and allow community interactions. Technical information supports operation, troubleshooting, security and understanding how the app is used.

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, the operator will use information as needed to provide the requested service, meet legal obligations, protect the service where permitted, or with your consent where required. Optional analytics or another optional purpose must not be treated as automatically authorized by agreeing to the Terms.

## 4. Who can receive information

Other users can see the profile and community content made available to them by the app. Your password is not part of your public profile. The operator must verify database access rules before claiming that any other field is private.

Google Firebase provides authentication, cloud database, media storage and related services used by SPOT. Information is processed by these providers to deliver their services under the applicable service terms. Provider processing may take place in countries other than yours. See Google's Firebase privacy information at https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

The operator may disclose information where reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to a valid legal request, investigate abuse, protect rights or safety, or carry out a properly notified business transition. Proposed policy: the operator does not sell personal information. This commitment must be confirmed before adoption.

## 5. Retention and deletion

Account, profile and community data are kept for the purposes described above while they are needed. A final retention schedule must identify [RETENTION PERIODS FOR ACTIVE ACCOUNTS, INACTIVE ACCOUNTS, LOGS AND BACKUPS]. The operator should avoid keeping personal information indefinitely without a defined need.

Request deletion through caybinmicdahryl@gmail.com. The operator may request proportionate identity verification, then delete the account and associated information unless a documented legal, security or other lawful retention reason applies. Requests will be handled within [CONFIRMED RESPONSE AND DELETION TIMEFRAME]. The final policy must explain any exceptions and backup expiry periods.

Removing SPOT from your phone does not remove data stored in Firebase. Copies other users have independently saved may remain outside the operator's control. An account deletion request must cover relevant authentication, database and storage data; deleting only the login record is insufficient.

## 6. Your choices and rights

You can choose whether to add an optional photo or biography and what you share publicly. Use the account recovery feature if you lose access. Contact caybinmicdahryl@gmail.com to request access, correction, a copy, deletion, withdrawal of consent or other rights available under applicable law. We may need to verify your identity and explain any lawful limits on a request.

You may also complain to the data protection authority with jurisdiction over your information. If Philippine data protection law applies, this includes the National Privacy Commission. Withdrawing consent affects future processing based on that consent and does not automatically invalidate earlier lawful processing.

## 7. Security

SPOT uses Firebase services and access controls to help protect information. The operator must maintain appropriate database and storage rules, secure access credentials and respond to security incidents. No system can promise complete security. Never share your password or include it in a support request.

## 8. Younger users

SPOT is intended for students and Spotters aged 16 and older. It is not intended for children under 16. Users aged 16 or 17 should review the policy with a parent or legal guardian, and any permission required by applicable law must be obtained. The signup acknowledgment is a self-declaration of age, not a verified date-of-birth check.

Young users should avoid posting their home address, precise whereabouts, class schedule, government documents or financial details. The operator must establish appropriate eligibility controls, reporting and any required guardian process before public launch. Contact caybinmicdahryl@gmail.com to report an underage account or raise a concern about a young person's information.

## 9. Policy changes and contact

When data practices materially change, the operator will update this policy and provide notice or request consent where required. Questions, privacy requests and complaints should be sent to caybinmicdahryl@gmail.com.
